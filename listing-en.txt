App name (30 characters max):
Rahma Cart

Short description (80 characters max):
Serve food and drinks to the wacky creatures of the Rahma world!

Full description:
🛒 Welcome to Rahma Cart!

You run a food cart in the Rahma world, and your customers are the Rahma creatures themselves: Hafozleeq, Aaaoot, Hankalees, Abu El-Zool, The Prince and many more. Every dish and drink is drawn in their likeness 😄

🌯 Cook fast
Wrap sandwiches, pour juice, fry potatoes and brew tea. Each customer's order is drawn next to them, and the heart under it is their patience. Serve in a row without mistakes to build combos.

🗺 From the alley to outer space
Start in Rahma Alley, then the Corniche, the stadium, all the way to the Rahma Space Station. Every place has its own customers and prices.

🎉 No two days alike
Rain and heat waves, power cuts, football matches, inspector raids, weddings, Ramadan and Eid, and midnight shifts with strange customers 👻. Sometimes your rival Sekertaout even opens a cart right across the street!

📱 Customers review you
At the end of each day customers post reviews on RahmaBook, each in their own voice. Good reviews bring more customers tomorrow.

🏪 Grow your business
Buy new dishes and add-ons, install upgrades, hire helpers, turn your cart into a kiosk and then a shop, and open a second branch.

🧸 Figures and the showcase
Buy a figure of every character and show it in your showcase. Sell their keychains to customers, or give them away free to become friends faster.

📖 Album and story
Befriend 16 characters, unlock their cards and side stories, and help Abu Hasiba pay off his debt to Sekertaout.

🏆 Daily challenge
The same customers for every player each day, so send your score to friends and see who wins. Finish the story to unlock Endless mode.

☁️ Save your progress with a Google account and continue on any phone (optional).
🌐 Fully playable in Egyptian Arabic or English.
🚫 No ads and no purchases.
